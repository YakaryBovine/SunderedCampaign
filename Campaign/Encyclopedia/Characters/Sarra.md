> [!NOTE] Player character
> Sarra was a player character played by Mark.

Sarra was a savage warrior that adventured with the Second Adventuring Party. She was a ferocious melee fighter, forgoing armour in favour of her natural talents, and preferring to wield two handaxes. She was also capable of shapeshifting - the result of an unfortunate curse that occasionally befell members of her tribe, and the reason for her exile.

During the party's [[Fratricidal Incursion|encounter]] with Hroth-Vaelor, Sarra recognized the dire situation they were in and quaffed the Potion of Infinite Strength. With its power, she was able to salvage the battle and defeat Hroth. The potion's powers, however, began to destroy her body, as her ever-growing muscles consumed her whole. She abandoned her gear, and ran into the wilderness to die.
