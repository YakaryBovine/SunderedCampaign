> [!NOTE] Player character
> Sarra was a player character played by Than.

Gilgamesh is a plucky young lad from a Shattered Expanse farming village blessed with high-tech hydroponic equipment from before the war. He grew up with a strong sense of community, and left his village with the blessings of his extended family and a strong wish to see him return one day.

When the Second Adventuring Party rescued a number of Stalradian-descended wastelanders from the demons of the Bloodwake, Gilgamesh felt duty-bound to give them a home. He retired with them to the hills of Draumheim, and called his new village [[Sarra|Sarra's Rest]] in honour of his fallen friend.
