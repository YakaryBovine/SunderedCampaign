Skofnildr is the secret God of the Stålråd, lord of Rejecting Divinity for Mundane Trinkets. He exists in derision of the other Gods and is derided by them all; he is the Stålråd refusal to bow to the heavens, and their fundamental interest in braving the future without divine assistance. The Urzithans reject his existence, and the Stålråd have never discovered it.

He is a hulking, shuffling mass of haphazard technology in the shape of a man, four meters tall and draped in a conductor's brown cloak. His upper face is that of a CRT screen, from which springs forth a magnificent snowy beard filled with an array of amusing trinket. His voice alternates between that of a kindly old man to a blend of machine error logs. When he signs, he exales a wave of dry, warm furnace air.
## The Conductor
You first met Skofnildr, in his capacity as a train conductor, when you boarded the Valdishal Grand Line. Upon departure, his "eternal nightmare" trapped you in a twelve minute time loop which always ended in the train exploding at the hands of sabotage. In the final loop, you dealt with the saboteurs, and arrived at your destination.
## The Betrayed
You learned Skofnildr's true nature when you [reached the bottom secret floor](The Fourth Sect) of a factory housing a forgotten chapter house of the Fourth Sect. Metal-engraved murals on the walls spun the story of his origin, retold below...

>Long ago, before civilization, a small girl invented the first piece of technology made without divine assistance: a Levallois core. The Spirits were fascinated.
>
>One Spirit was more fascinated than the rest, and he became a God. All this God wanted was to witness the invention of interesting trinkets and, perhaps, to collect them. But he could not; as a God, he was bound to the Spirit World, and as a Spirit, he could not understand technology no matter how hard he tried. 
>
>So he made a choice. He left the world of Gods to live in the Material, and in so doing became the God of Rejecting Divinity for Mundane Trinkets. 
>
>He roamed the world for millennia, collecting what trinkets he could and storing them in his infinitely deep beard. He could not hide from mortals, for his arrival was always heralded by a roiling cyan mist and his telltale furnace sigh. He was hounded constantly by his family, Khalumet, Ashur-Namta, and Urhat-Zel, who every year tried to give him the gift of true divinity. He always rejected them. 
>
>One day he discovered the Stalrad. They had wondrous trinkets like he had never seen, and he loved them. But he knew they would hate him, so he kept to the shadows, only picking up their scraps. 
>
>Then the Last War began. He knew the Stalrad would not survive against his brothers and sister of the Triumvirate. As the God of Rejecting Divinity, he chose to intervene, and suppressed the powers of the Triumvirate for near the entire war. 
>
>The Triumvirate were furious. They spent years coming up with a plan to defeat Him. And so they visited us, here, in His temple. We swear we didn’t know.

You met a simulacrum of Skofnildr at the end of the muralled hallway, and you gave him a copy of a device that had been fabricated by robotic avatars of the Triumvirate. This drove the simulacrum mad, and caused his death - but not before he revealed to you that he [misses his siblings](Three And One), and that the real version of him could be saved from madness by retrieving the real version of the device from deep within his beard.
## The Madman
You returned to Skofnildr at the Valdishal Grand Line, and delved within his beard to reach his divine realm, Null Storage. You persisted through the realm's technological dangers to reach the epicenter, a crash site where the device had fallen. There, Skofnildr in his maddened state tried to intervene, begging you not to destroy the device. You reached the center and disassembled it, freeing Skofnildr of his insanity.
## The God
Now of clear mind, Skofnildr took you aboard the Valdishal Grand Line and drove you through the earth, the ocean, and orbit. There, he showed you that the Stalrad high command [was still alive on the far side of the moon](Iron Moon), and that they would awaken in time. He also showed you that [divine magic is no longer being replenished](Divine Ebb). He then returned you to Hospice, where you discovered from above that a great ash storm was now covering the southern Shattered Expanse.

Before you left, he handed you a train whistle which you could use to summon Skofnildr and the Valdishal Grand Line to your location.
