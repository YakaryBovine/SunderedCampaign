> [!NOTE] Player character
> Akira was a player character played by Kieran.

Akira was a member of the Second Adventuring Party. He hailed from a small nation on the edge of the Shattered Expanse, founded by warrior-conquerors from another continent, its existence just barely tolerated by the Urzithans and the Stalrad.

As a dedicant of the Kami, the spirits of his people, he was able to channel their magics with charisma, voice, and rhythm. Combined with his skill in the blade, he was a deadly force in combat. A former military leader, he was able to assert himself in any situation.

When the party discovered that [[Divine Ebb|the world was losing its Divine spark]], he requested and received the patronship of the Collector Sabit, and retired to find a solution to this dire problem.
