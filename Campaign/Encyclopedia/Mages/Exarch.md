Exarchs are individuals who have committed a singular act of such extraordinary goodness that their deed, upon being witnessed by a curious Spirit, has transformed that Spirit into a stillborn Celestial. With no body of their own, the infant Celestial merges with the exalted individual, and in so doing forms an Exarch.

Being possessed of a Celestial grants an Exarch an intuitive ability to employ [[Divine]] magic, but because this magic is derived from within rather than without, their ability to understand and master it is limited. The precise nature and strength of an Exarch's magic is modelled primarily after the deed that granted them their abilities, and the good deeds they have committed since.

Exarchs are extraordinarily rare. When they do arise, they typically go on to have a profound impact on the world.

The actual use of the term "Exarch" is practically unheard of outside of scholarly circles. Typically, an Exarch would be merely thought of as a "hero" or "chosen one", or might be more closely associated with the military tradition they hail from, such as the samurais or paladins.
