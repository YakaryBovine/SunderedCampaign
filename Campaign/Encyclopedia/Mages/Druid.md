Druids are magical practitioners who have achieved mastery over the primal forces of nature. Druidic powers are ultimately derived from the Primals - the spirit embodiments of the world's natural forces - but this relationship is rarely conscious, and never mutual.

Instead, Druids build up their connection through a genuine connection to an understanding of nature. In so do doing they build up two powers: the magical, through their increasing relationship with the Primals, and the mundane, through their mastery of what nature has to offer them.

Druidic powers are myriad. Most commonly, they involve the manipulation of the elements, flora, or fauna. More rarely, they the Druid the ability to take animalistic or entirely-animal forms.

Most people have heard of Druids, and most people in rural environments will encounter at least one in their lives. It is uncommon, but not unheard of, for a Druid to venture into a dense urban city.

> [!NOTE] Druids in Worlds Without Number
> Druidic magic is varied, so there are a number of ways to play one using the Worlds Without Number Rules. [[Elementalist]] is the best fit for a spellcasting Druid, while [[Skinshifter]] allows shapeshifting and [[Beastmaster]] entails mastery over animals. [[Expert]] could be used to reflect a Druid's mundane knowledge of nature.
