Divine magic is the subset of the reality-warping power known as magic conferred upon mortals by Gods and Celestials. It is most commonly used to heal, enhance, or support, or to diminish or destroy profane entities, but the full limits of Divine magic extend to most possibilities.

It is very difficult for mortals to acquire and use divine magic. Most practitioners are [[Priests]], who learn it through years of study - those who understand their chosen Gods on a deep level are granted some of their powers. Less common are the [[Exarchs]], who gain their abilities by exemplifying the most vital aspects of their God.

Your relationship with Divine magic is likely myriad. If you're Stalradian, you hate it for how it elevates Gods amongst men and the mastery of material knowledge. If you're Urzithan, you may revere it and its practitioners as your leaders demand that you would, or you may fear it for the atrocities you've seen committed with it. If you're a wastelander, you may or may not have even witnessed Divine magic before.
